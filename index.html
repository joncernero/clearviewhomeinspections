Common questions

What is a home inspection?

A home inspection is a thorough visual review of the existing condition of a home's structure, craftsmanship, electrical and mechanical systems by a licensed Indiana home inspector.

How long does a home inspection take?

Most Clear View inspections take 2 to 3 hours, depending on the size, age and condition of the home.

Can I be present for the inspection?

Yes, and we encourage it. This is your inspection. Walk the house with your inspector, ask questions, and see issues firsthand.

When will I get my report?

Within 24 hours, and usually the same day. You get a detailed, easy-to-read report with digital photos, and issues are grouped by severity.
