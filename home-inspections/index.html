Full home inspections in Central Indiana

A Clear View home inspection is a visual inspection of the home's structure, construction and mechanical systems by a licensed Indiana home inspector. It takes 2 to 3 hours, you're welcome to attend, and your photo report arrives within 24 hours, usually the same day.

What we inspect

Indiana's minimum standards for home inspectors list the systems a licensed inspector must inspect and report on[1]:

SystemExamples of what's covered
StructureFoundation and framing, probed where deterioration is suspected
ExteriorWall coverings, doors, decks, railings, eaves, walkways, driveways, vegetation affecting the building
RoofCoverings, drainage, flashing, skylights, chimneys, penetrations
PlumbingWater supply and distribution, fixtures, drain/waste/vent, water heating, fuel systems, sump pumps
ElectricalService equipment, main disconnect, grounding, panels, conductors, overcurrent devices, representative lights/switches/receptacles, GFCIs
Heating & coolingInstalled equipment and vents, energy source and method
InteriorWalls, ceilings, floors, stairs, railings, countertops, representative cabinets, doors, windows, garage doors
Insulation & ventilationInsulation and vapor retarders in unfinished spaces, attic and foundation ventilation
FireplacesSystem components, vents and chimneys

What a standard inspection doesn't cover

Indiana's standards don't require inspectors to test for radon, mold or pests, inspect wells or water quality, or judge code compliance[1]. That's why we offer those as separate services: radon testing, mold testing, termite/WDI inspections, and well & septic certifications.

Your report

Indiana requires the report to identify systems that are "significantly deficient or near the end of their service lives," with reasons and recommendations[1].
