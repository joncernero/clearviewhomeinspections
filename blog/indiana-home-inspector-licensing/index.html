How are home inspectors licensed in Indiana?

By Clear View Home Inspections · Published September 24, 2026 · Last reviewed September 24, 2026

Indiana home inspectors must be licensed by the state's Home Inspectors Licensing Board. To get licensed, an inspector needs a board-approved course, a passing score on the National Home Inspector Examination, and at least $100,000 of general liability insurance. To keep the license, they need 32 hours of continuing education each two-year cycle[1]. You can verify any license online in about two minutes[5].

What it takes to get licensed

According to the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, an applicant needs[1]:

  • A high school diploma or GED
  • A board-approved pre-licensing training course
  • A passing score on the National Home Inspector Examination
  • General liability insurance of at least $100,000, with the Home Inspectors Licensing Board listed as certificate holder

Staying licensed

Licenses expire October 1 of every odd-numbered year. Inspectors licensed for 24+ months need 32 continuing education hours per renewal (16 if licensed 12–24 months). At least 75% must be technical (Category I) topics[1].

Who oversees inspectors

Indiana's Home Inspectors Licensing Board has seven governor-appointed members: four licensed home inspectors, a home builder, a real estate professional and a public member[2]. The board adopts rules, issues licenses and investigates complaints. It's also required to set performance standards and a code of ethics[2]. Those appear as Indiana's minimum standards of practice[3] and code of ethics[4].

What the license requires on your inspection

Under 878 IAC 1-2-1, a licensed inspector must inspect specific systems (structure, exterior, roof, plumbing, electrical, heating, cooling, interior, insulation/ventilation and fireplaces). The report must include the inspector's name and license number[3]. Radon, mold and pests are outside the required scope[3]. Radon testing takes a separate certification from the Indiana Department of Health[6].

How to verify an inspector in two minutes

  1. Ask for their Indiana home inspector license number. It must appear on the report anyway[3].
  2. Look it up on the state's Search & Verify site[5] and confirm it's active.
  3. If you're adding radon, ask for the IDOH radon tester license as well[6].

Questions worth asking before you book

  • How long have you inspected homes in this area, and how many a year?
  • Can I attend, and how long will it take?
  • When will I get the report, and can I see a sample?
  • Which add-ons (radon, termite, sewer, mold) do you offer, and are you certified for them?

Quick answers

Do home inspectors need a license in Indiana?

Yes. Indiana licenses home inspectors through the Home Inspectors Licensing Board, administered by the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency.

How do I check an Indiana home inspector's license?

Search the inspector's name on the state's Search & Verify site at mylicense.in.gov.

Does a home inspector license cover radon testing?

No. Radon testers need their own certification from the Indiana Department of Health.
