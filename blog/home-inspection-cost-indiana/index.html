How much does a home inspection cost in Indiana?

By Clear View Home Inspections · Published September 24, 2026 · Last reviewed September 24, 2026

A standard home inspection usually costs about $200–$500, depending mainly on the home's size and age[1]. Indianapolis-area prices sit in that range: one local company's 2026 list runs from $325 to $625 by square footage[2]. Radon, termite, mold and sewer scope testing are extra, because Indiana's standard inspection doesn't include them[3].

Typical cost by home size

Size is the biggest driver of price. National ranges[1]:

Home size (sq ft)Typical national range
Under 1,000$200–$250
1,001–1,500$250–$300
1,501–2,000$300–$400
2,001–2,500$400–$450
2,501–3,000$450–$500+

Local prices are in the same range. One Indianapolis inspection company's 2026 price list starts at $325 for homes up to 1,000 sq ft and runs up to $625 for 7,001–8,000 sq ft. It adds $50 for homes over 50 years old and $125 for duplexes[2].

Add-ons that change the total

Indiana's standards of practice don't require inspectors to test for radon, mold or pests, or to inspect wells and water quality[3]. Those services are priced separately:

What you get for the fee

A licensed Indiana inspection covers the structure, exterior, roof, plumbing, electrical, heating, cooling, interior, insulation and ventilation, and fireplaces. The report has to flag systems that are "significantly deficient or near the end of their service lives."[3] Knowing about one failing roof or furnace before closing usually costs far less than finding it after.

Is it worth it if I'm on a budget?

HUD's buyer notice puts it plainly: "The purpose of a home inspection is to inform and educate you about the property before you make a financial commitment." It also says FHA "does not guarantee the value or condition of your new home"[4]. An appraisal protects the lender. An inspection protects you.

Quick answers

How much is a home inspection in Indiana?

Most standard inspections fall within the national range of about $200–$500, based mainly on square footage and age. One Indianapolis company publishes 2026 prices from $325 (up to 1,000 sq ft) to $625 (7,001–8,000 sq ft). Radon, termite, mold and sewer scope cost extra.

Is radon testing included in a home inspection?

No. Indiana's minimum standards don't require inspectors to test for radon, mold or pests, so these are priced as add-ons.

Can I skip the inspection if the appraisal is fine?

HUD says an appraisal estimates value for your lender and does not replace a home inspection.
