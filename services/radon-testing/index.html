Radon testing in Fishers, Indianapolis & Central Indiana

Nearly 1 in 3 Indiana homes is estimated to have radon above 4.0 pCi/L, the level where EPA recommends fixing the home[1]. You can't see or smell radon. Testing is the only way to know, and it's easiest to schedule alongside your home inspection.

How it works

  1. We place a radon monitor in the lowest livable level when we do your inspection.
  2. The monitor runs for the test period while the home stays closed up.
  3. You get the result, and if it's at or above 4.0 pCi/L, next steps you can take into negotiations.
